Zero-Discrepancy Label Consistency Guarantee

The localization mask pixels strictly equal the summation of all 4 damage class pixels: Σ(Dmg 1 + Dmg 2 + Dmg 3 + Dmg 4) = Loc Binary (257,973 px train, 76,500 px test) . This ensures zero target misalignment leakage during Siamese cross-attention feature aggregation.
